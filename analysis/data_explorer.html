02

Rate versus mass: where the poor people actually are

Supported · strongest story

mpi_rank orders states by rate; mpi_poor_rank orders them by number of poor people (mpi_poor_thousands). The two orderings are close overall (rank correlation 0.94) with one decisive exception: Kano is 11th by MPI but 1st by mass, at 7.3 million poor people. Kano, Katsina, Bauchi and Jigawa hold 26.5 million of the 75.3 million poor people across the 37 units — 35% of the total from four states. A slope chart between the two rankings, or a scatter of rate against headcount, replaces 'poorest 12 states' with 'where the poor actually live'.

In Tableau: Scatter: mpi on X, mpi_poor_thousands on Y, state on detail. Or a bump chart with mpi_rank and mpi_poor_rank on a dual axis. Both fields already exist in the extract.

03

The dimension mix, stacked to 100%

Supported · decomposition

contrib_health_pct + contrib_education_pct + contrib_living_standards_pct sum to 100 for every row. A 100% stacked bar per state, sorted by MPI, shows the mix shifts with poverty — but not the way you would guess: education's share rises with MPI (r = 0.76) while health's share falls (r = -0.57), and living standards' share is flat (r = 0.04). Yobe draws 47% of its MPI from education and only 6% from health; Adamawa and Taraba are the reverse, 58% and 56% living standards. Caveat: health is child mortality alone and is re-weighted to a full third, so the mix is not cross-country comparable.

In Tableau: Measure Names / Measure Values on a stacked bar; Percentage of Total on the axis. Colour the three contributions with three assigned colours, then sort states by MPI descending.

04

Incidence x intensity quadrants

Supported

headcount_ratio_pct against intensity_pct, split at the 37-state median, with quadrant as the colour and a reference line at each median. It separates 'many people, shallow deprivation' from 'fewer people, deep deprivation' — two different policy problems with the same MPI. Be honest about the cells: H spans a factor of 68 across these states while A spans only 1.6, so the median cut produces a lopsided 17 / 2 / 2 / 16, and the quadrant names are about incidence and intensity, never 'high' and 'low' poverty.

In Tableau: Scatter plus two reference lines (medians of H and A); colour on the string field quadrant. No table calculation required.

07

What survives multiple-comparison correction

Supported · cautionary

16 states look locally unusual before correction; exactly 1 (Yobe) survives Bonferroni across 37 tests, and 0 survive Benjamini-Yekutieli. A paired bar of lisa_p vs lisa_p_bonferroni per state, or a simple two-bar chart of the counts, tells the reader how much of a cluster map is signal and how much is 37 simultaneous tests.

In Tableau: Bar chart of lisa_p and lisa_p_bonferroni side by side per state, plus a reference line at 0.05. Fields are in spatial_diagnostics.csv.

08

Conflict exposure against poverty

Fragile · undetectable

Spearman rho by round: +0.34 (2013), -0.20 (2016), +0.10 (2018), -0.06 (2021). Power at n = 37 is 0.75 only at |rho| = 0.4, nothing survives Bonferroni, and in 2021 five of the poorest 12 states record zero UCDP events. If it is drawn at all, it must be drawn with the zero-event states visible and labelled -- never as 'no relationship'.

In Tableau: Scatter of events_per_100k vs mpi faceted by year, with the zero-event states jittered onto the axis and counted in a footnote. A regression-free scatter; Tableau's trend line must stay off.

09

The climate gradient (and why it is a trap)

Fragile · confounded

Baseline precipitation correlates with MPI at rho = -0.80. But precipitation vs latitude is -0.903 and latitude vs MPI is +0.821, which is stronger. After accounting for latitude, Moran's I falls from 0.798 to 0.244, and after conflict and climate as well it is 0.108 (p = 0.107). Any climate view has to carry latitude in the same frame, otherwise it is a map of the north disguised as weather.

In Tableau: Scatter with latitude on an encoded axis or as a small-multiple dimension, and the second-order Moran's I printed in the subtitle.

10

Party alignment: beta with its interval, and the caveats beside it

Fragile · caveat required

beta = +0.0080 MPI points/year (95% CI -0.0002 to 0.0168, permutation p = 0.036): aligned states fell slower. The CI crosses zero, |beta| is below the minimum detectable effect (0.0115), it rests on 2013-16 alone, and the sitting-party check gives p = 0.079. If shown, the interval and the failure list must be in the same frame -- never a lone bar.

In Tableau: A dot-and-whisker per model row from party_alignment_result.csv (beta, ci_lo, ci_hi) with a zero reference line, faceted by kind. Text zone carries the caveat line.

11

Vulnerable and severe: two populations, not one scale

Supported · a genuine surprise in the data

severe_pct tracks the MPI almost perfectly (r = 0.99) — it is the same north-south story in a harsher cut. vulnerable_pct does not: r = -0.02 with MPI and -0.09 with severe poverty. The four highest vulnerable shares belong to Benue (35.0%), Cross River (34.7%), Ondo (32.4%) and Adamawa (31.2%), and none of those is in the poorest band. So 'at risk of falling in' and 'already in deep deprivation' are effectively different maps. That is worth showing precisely because the single MPI number hides it.

In Tableau: Scatter of vulnerable_pct (X) against severe_pct (Y), MPI on size, state on detail. Two reference lines at the medians give the four corners. Fields already present.
